Welcome

Let's get you set up — it takes about a minute.

The extension captures your job applications, but it keeps your data on a tiny desktop companion app that runs privately on your computer. Nothing leaves your machine. You just need to install it once.

  • Private — your applications, resumes, and answers stay on your PC.
  • Quiet — it lives in your system tray and captures jobs in the background.
  • Smart — fit scores, cover letters, and paced auto-apply, all local.
